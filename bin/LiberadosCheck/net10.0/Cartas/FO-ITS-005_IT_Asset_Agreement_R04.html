2.1 Use regulation

The following regulation must be accomplished by subscriber:

2.1.1 All IT Asset Equipment are property of Marquardt Mexico S. de R.L. de C.V and it may be re-assigned in accordance with the organization needs.
2.1.2 User must report immediately to IT Department any type of failure or damage that prevents the equipment to work properly.
2.1.3 It is totally prohibited to install any type of software without IT Department acknowledge or proper authorization. If needed, the subscriber must request it formally to OTRS/IT Support Portal in the proper way.
2.1.4 The user is fully responsible to back his/her labor information up into the Department drive (designed for such task). For help or guidance, customer can contact IT Department. (Personal data is not allowed over Department drives).
2.1.5 The customer is fully responsible for the assigned asset; therefore in case of failure or damage caused by inappropriate usage or negligence, the proper reparation could cause a sanction or fixed payment. Each case will be reviewed between IT, Finance and Human Resources departments in accordance to IT policies section 2.7.
2.1.6 In case of Virus, Trojan, Malware, etc., IT Department should provide the proper help to contain or fix the problem. However, IT Department is not responsible for information loss or downtime related.
2.1.7 In case of total loss of an IT asset, the customer must raise the proper notification at the local police department when applies and to the IT Department for immediate actions. For asset cost recovery determination, each case will be reviewed between IT, Finance and Human Resources departments, in accordance to IT policies section 2.6.
2.1.8 The use of communication hardware must accomplish Marquardt Conduct Code, if the user do not respect this code will be reported to their manager and also to Human Resources department.
2.1.9 The assignation will end if one of the following occurs:
• Organizational changes to the area of responsibility
• Authorization withdrawn by area manager.
• Termination or break of employment.
